## BRAND OVERVIEW
- Name: Vera Lane Cosmetics
- Hero product: Tone Adapting Foundation ("Color Changing Foundation")
- Tagline: "Adapts to your skin tone instantly"
- Voice adjectives: clean, confident, effortless, warm, trustworthy
- Positioning: a single white foundation that self-adjusts to the wearer's skin
  tone — removing the "wrong shade" problem. Premium clean-beauty, but
  approachable (not clinical, not fussy).

## VISUAL SYSTEM
- Headline / wordmark font: elegant high-contrast serif (Didone-style, à la Bodoni/Didot)
- Body / claims font: clean geometric sans-serif (à la Futura / Montserrat)
- Palette: warm neutrals — soft cream `#F4EEE6`, warm beige `#E7D8C6`, warm taupe
  `#B9A48E`, near-black `#161310` for type/cap. Accent: muted rose/nude.
- Background colours: cream / soft beige / linen, occasionally clean white for packshots.
- CTA style: solid near-black button, white sans-serif text.

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION
- Lighting: soft diffused natural daylight, gentle directional from camera-left,
  no harsh shadows.
- Colour grading: warm, lightly muted, soft contrast (never clinical or cold).
- Composition: generous negative space, editorial framing, 50mm look, shallow
  depth of field.
- Subjects: real, relatable women with healthy luminous skin and visible natural
  skin texture (NO plastic/AI sheen). For the current Gen-Z/millennial reposition,
  skew younger (18–35) and age-diverse rather than the prior 40+ framing.
- Props & surfaces: marble, linen, ceramic, fresh botanicals, soft fabrics.
  AVOID: clutter, plastic props, neon colours, busy backgrounds, harsh studio strobes.
- Mood: warm, calm, premium, effortless.

## PRODUCT DETAILS — Tone Adapting Foundation   ← MOST IMPORTANT FOR ACCURACY
- Physical: tall rectangular **frosted-glass** bottle, flat front face, squared
  shoulders; slim and elegant proportions (~30 ml dropper/pump bottle look).
- Contents colour: **PURE WHITE cream formula** — this is the signature feature
  (the white "colour-changing" base that adapts on skin). NOT beige, NOT tan,
  NOT skin-coloured. Models get this wrong constantly — always negate it.
- Cap/closure: **matte black, tall square flat-top** cap (rectangular block),
  matte finish, no detailing.
- Label/logo placement (printed directly on the frosted bottle, dark charcoal/black):
  1. "VERA LANE" — high-contrast serif wordmark, letter-spaced, upper-middle of bottle.
  2. "TONE ADAPTING FOUNDATION" — bold sans-serif, two lines, lower third.
  3. "ADAPTS TO YOUR SKIN TONE INSTANTLY" — small sans-serif caps, beneath the name.
- Distinctive features: frosted glass + matte black square cap + the white formula
  visible through the glass. No other logos or marks anywhere.
- Packaging system: sold as the single bottle; optional makeup brush as an add-on.

## AD CREATIVE STYLE

- Text overlay: minimal, serif headline + sans-serif sub.
- Trust stack: press logos (Cosmopolitan, Grazia, Elle, Vogue), star ratings,
